## 1. Before Sending an Invitation

- Does this meeting really need to happen?
- Who truly needs to attend?
- Can this be solved asynchronously instead?

---

## 2. Before Accepting an Invitation

- Do I need to be there?
- Can I contribute meaningfully?
- Could I get the outcome another way?

---

## 3. Before the Meeting Starts

- Why am I here?
- What value am I bringing?
- Would my time be better spent elsewhere?

---

## 4. Before Leaving the Meeting

- What did I learn?
- What action am I taking next?
- Was this meeting worth the time?
